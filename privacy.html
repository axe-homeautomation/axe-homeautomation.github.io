Privacy Policy

Home Automation Platform.

Back to the app · Terms of Service

Short version: your data stays local. This platform operates entirely client-side — there is no backend server collecting or storing your smart home data. Device information, automations, and configurations remain on your private network.

Your data

Smart device names, room layouts, automation rules, sensor readings, and any other data from your home are stored locally in your browser — typically in local storage or IndexedDB. No information about your devices, rooms, or routines is transmitted to our servers or third parties for storage or processing.

What we collect

  • Browser analytics via Google Analytics (see below). This includes approximate location, device type, browser, and page navigation to understand how the platform is used — it does not include any data about your home or devices.
  • Standard web server logs from your hosting provider (such as IP address and requesting pages) under their own privacy policy. The platform does not collect or access those logs.

Local storage

The platform stores in your browser:

  • Your home configuration — rooms, devices, and their groupings.
  • Automation rules you create.
  • Interface preferences (e.g., theme or layout choices).

All of this data lives exclusively in your browser. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Third parties

  • Your device manufacturers — Your Home Automation Platform communicates directly with your smart devices on your private network. Each manufacturer has its own data policies for any cloud service that device may use independently of this platform.
  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to measure how the platform is used, as described above. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Your control

Because all your home data resides in your browser, clearing your browser's local storage removes it. You can do this through your browser settings at any time without contacting us.
